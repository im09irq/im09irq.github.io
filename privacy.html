Privacy Policy - Generator Manager

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Data

All app data (subscribers, payments, expenses) is stored locally on the user's device. We operate no servers and do not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal data.

Google Drive

When the user links a Google account, the app requests only the drive.file scope, which allows access solely to backup files the app itself creates in the user's own Drive. The app cannot see any other files. Google user data is used only to save and restore the user's backups and is never shared with third parties. Access can be revoked at any time from the app or from the Google Account settings.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
